# Research assumptions and validation register

The grant proposal contains strong performance and physics claims that are **not experimentally demonstrated** by the supplied document.

| Claim or hypothesis | Validation required |
|---|---|
| Sub-picosecond end-to-end latency | Include sources, detectors, conversion, control and IO; distinguish optical transit from inference latency. |
| 1,000–100,000x energy efficiency | Compare wall-plug joules/inference at matched workload, accuracy and throughput. |
| Passive VO2 nonlinear activation | Measure optical pump requirements, switching dynamics, thermal effects, hysteresis and endurance. |
| Thousands of WDM channels per waveguide | Validate spectral bandwidth, channel spacing, noise, thermal drift and cross-talk. |
| Optical computing eliminates electronic bottlenecks | Account for memory, programming, control, readout and O/E interfaces. |
| Ready-to-fabricate hybrid SiN/BTO/VO2 stack | Obtain actual PDK/foundry process and materials compatibility evidence. |

Do not claim an existing hardware demonstrator, measured benchmark, patent filing, grant award or applicant credentials without independently verifiable evidence. Verify current fellowship eligibility and application requirements against the official program before submission.
